# M14 — Two records are corrected before the phase reads them

**Depends on:** nothing.
**Discharges:** `F17` and `F19` in [findings.md](../findings.md).

Five records are open as findings for asserting things that are no longer true.
These two are the ones nothing later in this phase touches, so they are fixed
once, now. The other three wait for [M19](m19.md), and the reason is in that
file.

## Done means

- **`F17`** — the [decisions.md](../decisions.md) index is one row short of the
  file's `###` entries. The missing row is added. No entry is edited, and the unit
  states the count before and after so the fix is verifiable by anyone who doubts
  it.
- **`F19`** — [README.md](README.md) opens by saying M8–M13 are scoped and
  unstarted, and that `planned` and `deferred` are words its table has not used.
  Both were true when written and neither is now. The paragraph describes the
  phase it belongs to in the past tense, or is cut with the cut recorded.
- Both rows move to *Closed* with their dispositions.
- **Neither is a reopening.** No unit ever claimed a complete index, and the stale
  paragraph belongs to the phase-two plan rather than to any unit's definition of
  done. [triage.md](../triage.md) reopens a unit whose *claim* is false, and
  neither of these is one — which is worth stating, because a new rule gets
  applied too widely before it gets applied well.

## Risks

Low, and the risk is that it looks too low to think about. `F19` is the scope
contract's opening paragraph — the first thing anybody reads about what this
repository is doing — and the fix is a judgment about whether a phase plan is a
historical record that should be preserved in the past tense or a live document
that should be cut. Getting that wrong in either direction is cheap to correct
and sets a pattern for every phase after this one.
