# M9 — Conformance is defined narrowly, and stays self-asserted

**Depends on:** [M8](m8.md).
**Discharges:** the README's claim that "you write the checker, and the fixtures
tell you whether you got it right", which currently has no stated pass
criterion.

The fixtures are offered as the evidence and as the conformance suite. Nothing
anywhere says what passing them means. An implementer today can run all 33 trees,
match every expectation, and still not know whether they have written a
conforming checker or merely a checker that agrees with 33 examples.

## Done means

- `docs/conformance.md` exists and states the criterion: a checker conforms to
  a module at a version when, over every fixture tree of that module, it emits
  exactly the expected state for each check id — no extra findings, no missing
  ones — while honouring the five-state rule and writing nothing to the
  repository it audits.
- It states the three things conformance does **not** mean, in the same voice
  [README.md](../../../README.md) uses to refuse them: no badge, no list of
  conforming implementations, no record kept upstream of anyone who claims it.
  Conformance is self-asserted, and upstream holds nothing with which to
  contradict a false claim. That is a structural guarantee and it is also an
  accepted cost.
- The words *certified*, *certification*, and *compliant* do not appear as a
  status anywhere in it. `conforms to DL-03` is the permitted form and the file
  should model it — see [triage.md](../triage.md) standing rules.
- It states what partial conformance is, because that is the honest common case:
  a checker conforming to three modules and not the other two is useful, and a
  criterion that only recognises all-or-nothing pushes implementers to claim
  more than they have.
- It states that conformance is against a **pinned version**, and that a module
  releasing a new check makes prior conformance claims stale rather than false.
- Deliberately not done: no process for anyone to register, submit, or announce
  a conforming implementation. There is nowhere to send it and that is the
  design.

## Risks

This is the unit most likely to be a mistake. Every certification body in
existence began as a document explaining what passing meant, written by people
who would have said the same thing this file says. The defence is that upstream
holds no record of adopters, so there is nothing to certify against and nothing
to revoke — but that defence is structural rather than moral, and it survives
only as long as nothing here starts keeping a list.

If the judgment is that a conformance document is a step toward the thing
[README.md](../../../README.md) refuses, the correct outcome is to cut this unit
and let [M10](m10.md) compare against [M8](m8.md)'s expectation files alone. That
costs the implementer a stated criterion and costs the catalog nothing else.
**This is a decision for the owner and not a call this unit should make on its
own.**
