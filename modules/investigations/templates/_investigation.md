## Context

cost to get it wrong. A record with no stakes describes something that did not
need investigating.

If a finding contradicted what you expected, say so and say what the difference

expected" is usually the most valuable sentence in the record, and it only gets
written if the format leaves room for it.

## Decisions

The rules that follow from the findings above, numbered, each one something
somebody can actually follow. Keep these separate from the findings — an
observation stays true of what it was observed against, while a rule drawn from
it can stop being right, and merged they cannot be revisited independently.

## Reproducing

How to run this again. If the setup was deliberately not kept, say so and say
what it would take to rebuild it — the findings are the durable part and
pretending otherwise leaves the next reader to discover it themselves.
