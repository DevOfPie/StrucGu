# &lt;NNNN&gt; — &lt;a claim that can be true or false&gt;

For the `per-decision-files` form: one of these per decision, plus an index file
listing them.

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by a link to the record that
replaced this one

If this record is superseded, add the pointer above and **change nothing else**.
The rest of the text stays wrong, on purpose — it is the record that this was
believed, which is the thing worth keeping.

## Context

What the situation was and what made a decision necessary. State the stakes of
getting it wrong; a decision with no stakes did not need recording.

## Alternatives

What else was considered, and what each would have cost. A record with one
option is a record of something that was never actually decided.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated as a rule someone can follow.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it forecloses. Include the
costs — a record listing only benefits is advocacy written after the fact.
